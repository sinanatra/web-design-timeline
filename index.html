The disappearance of Web Design

This graph traces the evolution of the term "Web Design" in the literature from 1990 to the present day. The term has been in decline for years: the web itself has become more technical, more standardised, more off-the-shelf. Olia Lialina has criticised the idea underlying this change: that the less one thinks about code, the more "creative" one is.

The personalised, handcrafted pages of the early years of the web have therefore been gradually replaced by predefined templates. Nevertheless, in recent years, alternative micro-resistance movements have been re-evaluating the web in a personal and handcrafted way.
